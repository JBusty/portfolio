My process.

Five steps, every project, whether it's a security product or my own side builds. The line below runs on real work. Click it to send another idea through, or hover a step.Real projects don't always get the whole line. Deadlines move, teams shrink, pilots get cut. Here's how I keep the quality when the process breaks.

01Plan B

Discover

I talk to the people using it, and I read every support ticket I can find.

No time for research?

I spend a day in the support queue and get three customers on a call.

02Plan B

Design

I prototype early, test it with real people, and change my mind when they prove me wrong.

No time for polish?

I skip the polished mocks and prototype in code against real data.

03Plan B

Build

I prototype with AI, so ideas turn into working software in days, not sprints. I was a front-end developer first, so I can take it the rest of the way.

Short on engineers?

I build it myself with AI and ship the smallest version that works.

04Plan B

Validate

I get it in front of real customers before everyone gets it.

Can’t reach customers?

I go to the people who hear from them every day (support, sales and solutions engineers) and back it up with usage data.

05Plan B

Ship

I ship it, then check whether it actually helped.

Had to cut corners?

I ship it, then make sure the cleanup is on the roadmap, not just in my head.

Power source

Me, in a wheel

Fueled by coffee and curiosity. Steady pace, whatever the deadline.

Hamster down?

I get out and push. It isn’t pretty, but it ships.
